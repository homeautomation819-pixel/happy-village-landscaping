# Phase 2 — Competitor Gap Analysis
**Market:** North York / Toronto / GTA. Sources: live SERPs browsed during research (URLs cited per cluster).

## Cluster: Landscaping / landscape design (North York–Toronto)
Top rankers observed: UltraScape (ultrascape.ca/service-areas/north-york), Greenbay Northern, Hogan Landscaping, Precision Landscaping, plus HomeStars aggregator pages.

| They have X | We do Y | Our advantage Z |
|---|---|---|
| Service-area pages per suburb with thin, template-spun text | Location pages with unique content blocks, local FAQs, nearby-areas links | Passes duplicate-content checks; every suburb page reads as written for that suburb |
| No pricing anywhere; "call for quote" only | Published typical price ranges per service, confirmed at free quote | Captures cost-modifier searches and builds trust before the call |
| Landscaping-only scope | 16 services: outdoors + indoors + commercial + snow | One-call positioning nobody in the top 5 matches |
| Slow sites, heavy sliders, stock hero videos | <1MB pages, next/image WebP, system fonts | Core Web Vitals green = ranking tiebreaker + mobile conversion |
| Generic stock photos | Service-matched photography with keyword filenames + alt text | Image search visibility + accessibility + E-E-A-T |

## Cluster: Hardscaping / interlock (Toronto)

| They have X | We do Y | Our advantage Z |

## Cluster: Snow removal (North York / Toronto)
Top rankers: PROPERTYWERKS (North York page), Silverlight, HomeStars listings, franchise operators.

| They have X | We do Y | Our advantage Z |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing hidden or franchise call-centre flow | Seasonal vs per-visit pricing model explained, 24-hour availability stated | Transparency + "open 24 hours" GBP alignment |
| Winter-only operators | Year-round crew (lawn care summer → snow winter) | Same-crew continuity is a real, provable differentiator |
| Weak FAQ coverage | 5–7 PAA-style FAQs with FAQPage schema | Featured-snippet and AI-overview extractability |

## Cross-cutting gaps we exploit site-wide
1. **Cost transparency** — every service page carries "typical ranges, confirmed at quote" (grounded in live 2026 GTA sources).
2. **Schema depth** — 8 JSON-LD types site-wide vs competitors' 1–2.
3. **Location pages with unique copy** — competitors spin; we write per-suburb blocks (local landmarks, soil/permits, response time).
4. **Speed** — static-first Next.js, system fonts, WebP everywhere, no jQuery/sliders.
5. **NAP consistency** — one admin-driven source feeding footer, schema, llm.txt, contact page.
