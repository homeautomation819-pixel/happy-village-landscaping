## 4. SERP difficulty notes (from live results)
- **"landscaping North York/Toronto"**: dominated by established local firms with service-area page systems (UltraScape, Greenbay Northern, Hogan) + aggregators (HomeStars). Difficulty: medium-high. Gap: most lack transparent pricing and one-crew indoor+outdoor positioning.
- **"sod installation cost Toronto"**: blog SERP, several 2026 cost guides ranking. Difficulty: medium. Win with fresh, itemized, locally-grounded numbers.
- **"basement finishing cost Toronto"**: HomeStars price guide + contractor blogs with calculators. Difficulty: medium-high. Win with per-line-item breakdown and Toronto-specific permit notes.
- **"interlock driveway cost Toronto"**: multiple 2026 price guides ($18–$40/sq ft consensus). Difficulty: medium. Win with real line items (base depth, drainage, borders).
- **"snow removal North York"**: local operators + property-service franchises. Difficulty: medium. Gap: few publish pricing models (seasonal vs per-visit) clearly.
- **Long-tail reno keywords (drywall/flooring/painting + Toronto)**: mixed intent SERPs with room for a specialist page from a multi-trade local crew. Difficulty: low-medium.
